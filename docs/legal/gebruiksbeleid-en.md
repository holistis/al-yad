# Acceptable Use Policy — Yad

**Issued by:** Holistisch Adviseur, KvK 86816632, Bramantestraat, Eindhoven, the Netherlands.
**Version:** 1.0 — **Date:** September 11, 2026
**Abuse reports contact:** info@holistischadviseur.nl

> **Status of Yad.** Yad is free, runs locally, is in a concept/beta stage, and has no account or DPA relationship. This policy governs what is and is not allowed with the software regardless of that free/beta character, because the underlying risks (unauthorized access, deception, harm to third parties) do not change with the business model.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This Acceptable Use Policy (the "AUP") describes permitted and prohibited use of Yad, a browser automation agent that performs actions within your own, already logged-in browser session. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

## 2. Core principle: you remain responsible

Yad performs actions on your own instruction, within your own sessions. You are and remain responsible for every action you let Yad perform, including on third-party websites and systems.

## 3. Permitted use

You may use Yad for automation tasks on systems you are yourself authorized to access, within the bounds of those systems' own terms of use.

## 4. Prohibited use

You may not use Yad to:

**4.1 Unauthorized or unlawful access**
1. attempt to gain unauthorized access to systems, accounts, networks or data of third parties;
2. break or bypass security measures, use false signals or false credentials, or assume a false identity to gain access;
3. access a system against the clear or knowable will of the rightsholder;
4. act in violation of computer crime legislation (including Dutch Criminal Code article 138ab) or comparable foreign provisions.

**4.2 Bypassing security**
1. bypass, disable or undermine Yad's own safety filters, deny-list or confirm-before-act controls (article 6), regardless of intent;
2. bypass CAPTCHAs, bot detection, access controls or rate limits of a third party where doing so is unlawful or against that third party's policy.

**4.3 Violation of third-party rights**
1. violate the terms of use or account policy of a target system;
2. infringe intellectual property rights or database rights;
3. scrape personal data or protected databases without a valid legal basis, regardless of whether they are technically publicly accessible.

**4.4 Deception, fraud and spam**
1. deceive, defraud, phish, or unlawfully impersonate another party;
2. send spam or bulk communication in violation of the law;
3. generate dark patterns, fake clicks, or fraudulent metrics.

**4.5 Unlawful processing of personal data**
1. process personal data without a valid legal basis under the GDPR, or collect or otherwise process special category data (GDPR article 9) without an adequate legal basis.

**4.6 Payments, orders and irreversible actions without confirmation**
1. let Yad perform unattended actions with high or irreversible impact without your explicit, prior confirmation, including placing orders, making payments, and entering into legal or financial transactions;
2. bypass or disable the built-in deny-list or confirm-before-act controls (article 6).

**4.7 Disruption and harm**
1. distribute malware or harmful scripts;
2. overload, disrupt or take offline a system or infrastructure belonging to a third party.

## 5. Your own authorization

You represent that you are authorized to automate the systems on which you deploy Yad, that you hold the necessary rights and account authority, and that, to the extent you thereby process personal data of others, you yourself have a valid legal basis for doing so and meet your own duty to inform those individuals.

## 6. High-risk actions and the deny-list

Yad is not intended for fully unattended execution of actions with high or irreversible impact, such as payments, orders, and financial or legal transactions. A confirm-before-act requirement applies to such actions: your explicit, prior confirmation is required.

Yad has a built-in deny-list that blocks or requires prior confirmation for certain paths (including those related to paying, ordering and checkout). This safeguard is a technical aid, not a guarantee of completeness. You may not bypass or disable it, and you remain responsible for putting in place appropriate precautions for high-risk tasks.

## 7. Reporting and enforcement

If you suspect abuse or a violation of this policy, report it via info@holistischadviseur.nl. In the event of a violation, we may restrict use of Yad or, in the case of open source distribution, refuse access to supported services (such as future hosted features).

## 8. Changes

We may amend this policy. The current version is always available at yadagent.com.

## 9. Relationship to other documents

This Acceptable Use Policy forms part of our Terms of Service. In case of conflict, the interpretation that best serves the intent applies: no unauthorized, deceptive or harmful use is permitted, regardless of which document states that most clearly.
