2. What Yad is, and why there is little to tell

Yad is a Chrome extension plus a local Companion app that runs on your own computer. There is:

  • No account. You do not need to register or log in anywhere to use Yad. We do not keep a user database.
  • No payment. Yad is free. There is no billing, no subscription, no payment provider in this product.
  • No LLM contract of our own. Yad uses whichever language model you choose, with your own API key (for example Groq, Cerebras, Gemini or OpenRouter). That key and the associated data flow run directly between your browser and that provider. We are not a party to that flow: we do not choose the provider for you, we never see your key, and we do not receive the content of those exchanges on our own servers. Whatever privacy terms apply to that processing are set by the provider you chose; consult their own privacy policy.
  • No telemetry, no analytics, no cookies. We checked this against our own source code: there is no built-in measurement software in the extension or the Companion, and our website places no cookies or tracking scripts.

Because none of these usual collection points exist, Holistisch Adviseur does not currently process any personal data about you through Yad, beyond what is described in chapters 3 and 4 below.

3. What happens locally on your own device

As part of carrying out a task, Yad reads the content of the page currently open in your browser (the DOM), within your own, already logged-in session. This happens:

  • Locally only, and on your own instruction. The extension and the Companion run on your own computer. We have no access to what happens there and receive nothing from it.
  • Only to the extent needed to carry out the task you assigned. Yad does not read along beyond the purpose of that task.
  • May contain personal data visible on the automated page, of yourself or of third parties (for example if you assign Yad a task on a page showing someone else's data). As described above, that information goes to the language model you chose, using your own key. We never see this content.
  • You decide which pages and tasks you give to Yad. You are responsible for having a valid legal basis if you use Yad on pages containing personal data of others, for example in a business context.

Your API key is encrypted at rest on your own device (Windows DPAPI), never leaves your device to our servers, and can be stopped or removed with one click.

4. Our website (yadagent.com)

Our website places no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking software. Visiting the website results in the usual technical connection data processed by your browser and our hosting provider (such as IP address and timestamp), needed simply to serve the page. We do not use this data for our own purposes and do not retain it longer than our hosting provider's standard default.

5. Your rights

Even though we ourselves process little to no personal data about you, under the GDPR you in principle have the usual rights where applicable: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Send a request to info@holistischadviseur.nl; we respond within one month.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, we would like to hear that from you first. You also always have the right to file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

6. Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate for a free, locally running, concept-stage product: encryption of your API key on your own device, no server-side copy of your data, and an architecture deliberately designed to collect as little as possible. Because Yad is in a concept/beta stage, we do not offer guarantees appropriate for a mature, commercial product. Use is at your own risk, as also stated in our Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy.

7. Children

Yad is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16.

8. AI transparency

Yad is an AI system that reads text, generates text, and performs actions on your own instruction, within your own browser session. There is no interaction with third parties on your behalf that you did not instruct yourself.
