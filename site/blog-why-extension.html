Why Yad is a Chrome extension, not a new browser

2026 became the year of the AI browser. Here's where Yad actually stands in that field, and where it doesn't.

This year filled up fast: whole new browsers built around an AI agent (Atlas, Comet, Dia, Opera Neon), heavyweight extensions bundled into a browser you already use (Claude in Chrome, part of Claude Pro), and open-source code libraries you wire up yourself (browser-use, Skyvern, Stagehand).

The first category carries a real risk, not a hypothetical one. ChatGPT Atlas launched in October 2025. OpenAI retired it less than a year later, folding it back into the ChatGPT app in August 2026. Betting your daily browser on a brand-new standalone product means it can simply stop existing. That is not a knock on OpenAI, it is just what happened.

Yad picked a third path on purpose: a regular Chrome extension. You keep the browser, bookmarks, passwords, and other extensions you already have. No new browser to trust, no account, no subscription.

Model choice matters too. Claude in Chrome ties you to one company’s model behind one subscription. Yad does not choose the model for you, you point it at the AI you already use.

The closest comparisons in spirit are other open, privacy-first projects like Nanobrowser and BrowserOS. What is specifically different about Yad: the code is open, and so is what goes wrong with it.

We will say the honest part out loud too: we are not a large lab, and we have not run an industry benchmark at the scale browser-use has published (89 percent on WebVoyager). What we have published is smaller and on our own terms: a same-day comparison against browser-use on twelve tasks, and three separate write-ups of real failures with the exact fixes that followed. That is a smaller claim, and one we can actually stand behind, because we ran it ourselves and showed the losses too.

Short version: new browsers can disappear, big-lab extensions lock you into one model and a subscription, SDKs ask you to write code. Yad tries to be the boring, durable option: an extension on the browser you keep, no account, you choose the model, and we tell you when it breaks.
