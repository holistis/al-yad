How it fits together

Two small parts. Both yours. Both free.

Yad splits on purpose, so the thinking stays on your machine and your data stays with you.

THE HAND

The extension in Chrome

A light add-on that can see the page and do the clicking and typing. It has no brain of its own, so on its own it can do nothing.

THE BRAIN

A small app on your computer

It takes your task, thinks with the AI you picked, and tells the Hand what to do. Because it runs on your machine, your passwords and your pages never have to leave it.

You install the extension, then run the Brain once. That is the whole setup.

Why Yad is different

Built so your data stays your data.

Your logins stay yours

Yad works in your real, already logged in browser. Your passwords and card numbers are hidden before anything is sent. We do not store your data, and we never sell it.

You choose the AI

Pick a cloud model when you want the most power. Or pick a local model, and then nothing leaves your computer at all. Your call, per task.

Works on any site

No fake cloud browser, no copy of your accounts on someone else's server. Yad acts in the same browser you already use, so sites treat it like you.

Honest about the limits

What Yad does, and what it will not do.

Yad does

  • → Reads the page you are on, its text and links.
  • → Clicks, types, fills in forms, follows steps.
  • → Pauses and asks you before anything important.
  • → Learns to recover when a site trips it up.

Yad will not

  • ✕ Check out, pay, or place orders without you.
  • ✕ Store your browsing, your logins, or your files.
  • ✕ Send your passwords anywhere. They are masked first.
  • ✕ Sell or share your data. Not now, not ever.

When Yad gets stuck on a site, it can send one tiny anonymous note so it gets better next time: the site's domain and the type of hiccup. No page content, no logins, no personal data. You can turn this off in the settings.
